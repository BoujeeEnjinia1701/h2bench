# BOM notes

- Rows 1 to 15 are numbered to match the callouts in `media/exploded.png`. Rows 16 to 18 are not numbered in the exploded view; the gas lines of row 16 are modelled as 6 mm tubes.
- Every line is priced. All prices are indicative USD figures for a single prototype in 2026, not quotes. Items 6, 9 and 12 are estimates for generic parts and carry the largest uncertainty. The branded 12 W fuel cell list price ($576) and the MSE 300 mL/min single-cell stack price ($1,396.95) were checked on 2026-09-25.
- Totals (checked by `docs/04-calcs/sizing.py`, HBN-CAL-001 v0.4 section 10): **USD 999** for all 18 lines; USD 934 if the school supplies the bench power supply (line 4); USD 914 if the bench socket is also already RCD protected (line 18).
- Value-engineering target: USD 885 (`budget_usd`, a hypothetical control target, not a limit; Amish, 2026-10-01). Estimated cost of the constructable design: USD 999 (USD 114 over the target). The main cost drivers and savings worth trying are in the design decisions register (`docs/06-design-decisions.md`).
- Lines 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 12, 16 and 17 were repriced on 2026-10-01 for the parts added to make the design buildable (HBN-DDR-003): drip lip angle and frame brackets (1); rear posts, top frame, brackets, flanged duct collar and outlet hanger (3); reservoir stand, water post and band clips (5); electrolyzer feet (6); column bracket, pipe clips and separator drain valve (7); arrestor saddle and clip (8); tank cradle, M10 guard rods, nuts, tee nuts and top plate (9); fuel cell bridge (12); water lines and the vent line (16); T-nuts, self-tapping screws and sealant (17). Together they add USD 114.
- The H2Guard detector, fan and interlock (line 15) is costed in the H2Guard project and is **not** included in the total. The bench must not run without it.
- Line 10's vent valve is a needle valve set so that venting 300 to 20 kPa gauge takes 3 min or more (HBN-CAL-001 section 7). Its relief valve is set to 325 kPa gauge, and it carries a pressure switch that opens at 310 kPa gauge; the switch drives the relay in line 14, which breaks the electrolyzer supply (HBN-DDR-002). These added $20 to lines 10 and 14.
- Line 18 (plug-in 30 mA RCD) was added at TRL 3 because the safety sections require RCD protection and no line covered it.
- The existing lab table shown in the renders is not part of the BOM.
- Decided on 2026-10-02 (HBN-DEC-001), not yet in the BOM lines or prices: the tank (line 9) is a certified aluminium cylinder (6061-T6 class), about 2 L, stamped working pressure 1 MPa or more, threaded neck (stainless only if a rated cylinder of about 1.0 kg or less is found); a catalytic deoxidizer is added between the separator and the drier; the deck becomes 10 mm; and a 24 V normally closed tank solenoid powered from H2Guard is proposed to H2Guard in place of the 12 V solenoid. The bench power supply stays in the kit.
